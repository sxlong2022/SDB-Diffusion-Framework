**Title:** Hybrid Spatio-Temporal Generative Diffusion Model for Satellite-Derived Bathymetry using Multi-Source Data Fusion

**(Alternative Titles):**
*   High-Resolution Satellite-Derived Bathymetry using a Spatio-Temporal Diffusion Model Conditioned on Multi-Source Observations
*   Fusing Sentinel-2, GEBCO, and Chart Data for Accurate Bathymetry Mapping via a Hybrid Conditional Diffusion Model

**Abstract:**
Accurate and high-resolution bathymetry is crucial for coastal management, navigation safety, and marine research. Satellite-Derived Bathymetry (SDB) offers a cost-effective alternative to traditional surveys, but faces challenges in accuracy and spatial coherence, particularly in complex coastal waters. Classic machine learning approaches often struggle with these challenges, while deep learning methods are still emerging in this domain. This paper proposes a novel hybrid spatio-temporal generative diffusion model (S3GM-Bathymetry) for SDB. Our approach uniquely integrates multi-source data by conditioning an S3GM, based on a U-Net video model architecture and a Variance Preserving Stochastic Differential Equation (VP-SDE) formulation with v-parameterization, on: (1) preliminary bathymetry estimates from a classic Random Forest (RF) model trained on Sentinel-2 imagery, (2) GEBCO priori depth data, and (3) sparse, high-accuracy nautical chart soundings. The conditioning is achieved through a guided sampling process incorporating data fidelity and spatial smoothness terms. We demonstrate the crucial role of hyperparameter tuning, particularly the SDE noise schedule (`beta_max`) and the data fidelity weight (`alpha`), for stable and accurate results. Validated against official nautical charts in our study area (specify location briefly, e.g., near Zhoushan Archipelago, China), the S3GM-Bathymetry achieves significantly higher accuracy (R² ≈ 0.937, RMSE ≈ 3.19 m) and superior spatial coherence compared to the baseline RF model. The results highlight the potential of conditional diffusion models for complex geospatial regression tasks involving multi-source data fusion in remote sensing.

**Keywords:** Satellite-Derived Bathymetry (SDB), Diffusion Models, Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM), Spatio-Temporal Modeling, Generative Models, Sentinel-2, Multi-Source Data Fusion, Hybrid Model, Coastal Remote Sensing, Machine Learning.
